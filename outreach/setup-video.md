# MailMind setup video: script

**What it's for:** send it with every invite link, pin it in Settings, and post
it where you share MailMind. It walks someone from the invite link to their
first list.

**Length:** about 2 minutes. **Format:** a screen recording with your voice over
it. Loom or QuickTime is fine; face cam optional, bottom corner.

**Before you hit record**

- Use a demo Gmail account (e.g. `mailmind.demo@gmail.com`), not your own.
  Every screen shows email addresses and subjects.
- Put a few believable emails in its inbox: a school field-trip form, a client
  asking for something by Thursday, a store promo, a newsletter.
- Make an invite code at `/admin/codes` for the demo account, e.g. `DEMO`.
- Set browser zoom to 125%, hide bookmarks, close other tabs, and turn on Do
  Not Disturb.
- Record each scene as its own clip. A retake then only costs one scene.

---

## Scene 1: the hook (0:00–0:12)

**Screen:** a messy Gmail inbox, scrolling. Cut to the sample list at
`mailmind.dev/sample`.

**Say:**
> "This is my inbox. And this is what MailMind sends me instead: just the
> handful of things that actually need me. Here's how to set it up. It takes
> about two minutes."

## Scene 2: open the invite (0:12–0:30)

**Screen:** paste the invite link (`mailmind.dev/i/DEMO`) into the address bar.
The page says *You're invited.* Click **Continue with Google**, pick the demo
account, and Google asks only for name and email. The page now says *Your
invite is ready* with the code filled in. Click **Unlock MailMind**.

**Say:**
> "Open your invite link and sign in with Google. Notice what Google asks for:
> just your name and email address. MailMind never gets your password or
> access to your inbox. Your code is already filled in, so click Unlock."

*If you're recording for people without an invite, show the free-trial path
instead: **Start your free week** → sign in → **Start free trial**.*

## Scene 3: your private address (0:30–0:42)

**Screen:** you land on Settings. Highlight the **Forwarding** card and the
address, then click **Copy**.

**Say:**
> "This is your private MailMind address. Anything you forward here becomes
> part of your list. Copy it."

## Scene 4: forward from Gmail (0:42–1:20)

**Screen:**
1. In the "Set up in Gmail" guide, click the
   **Gmail → Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP** link.
2. Click **Add a forwarding address**, paste, click **Next**, then **Proceed**.
3. Switch back to the MailMind tab and refresh. The **Gmail confirmation code**
   appears.
4. Copy it, go back to Gmail, paste it next to **Verify** and click it.
5. Choose **Forward a copy of incoming mail to** your MailMind address, with
   **keep Gmail's copy in the Inbox**. Scroll down and click **Save Changes**.

**Say:**
> "Click the link to open Gmail's forwarding settings, add a forwarding
> address, and paste. Gmail sends a confirmation code to MailMind, and
> MailMind shows it to you right here. Refresh if you don't see it yet. Copy
> the code back into Gmail, verify, then turn forwarding on, keeping Gmail's
> copy in your inbox. Nothing leaves your inbox; MailMind just gets a copy.
> Save."

## Scene 5: only some mail (1:20–1:35), optional but good for parents

**Screen:** in Gmail, open search options. Type the school's address or
domain (e.g. `from:(@yourdistrict.org OR parentsquare.com)`), click **Create
filter**, tick **Forward it to**, and pick your MailMind address.

**Say:**
> "Don't want to forward everything? Make a filter instead. I only send my
> kids' school email, and MailMind turns it into a list: forms to sign,
> things to bring, money to send."

## Scene 6: pick your time (1:35–1:50)

**Screen:** back in MailMind Settings, scroll to **Delivery**. Check your time
zone, pick a time (e.g. 7:00 AM, maybe 5:00 PM too), and click **Save
schedule**. The Forwarding card now says **Receiving**.

**Say:**
> "Last step: choose when your list shows up, up to three times a day. And
> see that: it says Receiving, so your mail is coming through."

## Scene 7: what you get (1:50–2:05)

**Screen:** open the sample list email (or a real one, blurred), then
`mailmind.dev/list` and check an item off.

**Say:**
> "At your time, you get one email like this: just what needs you, who it's
> from, and a calendar button when there's a date. It's on the web too, where
> you can check things off. That's it. Your inbox, as a to-do list."

**End card (3 sec):** `mailmind.dev` · "7 days free"

## A 30-second cut for social (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

Vertical, captions on, no narration needed:

1. **0–3s:** an inbox showing "1,284 unread". Caption: *"My inbox every
   morning."*
2. **3–8s:** fast scroll through promos and newsletters. Caption: *"Most of
   this doesn't matter."*
3. **8–18s:** the MailMind list email. Caption: *"MailMind sends me only what
   needs me."* Tap through the four items.
4. **18–25s:** the Gmail filter screen. Caption: *"Forward everything, or just
   school email. It never gets your password."*
5. **25–30s:** `mailmind.dev`. Caption: *"7 days free. Link in bio."*

Tag each place you post it with its own `?ref=` (e.g.
`mailmind.dev/?ref=tiktok`) so `/admin/analytics` shows which one works.

## Where people get stuck (for the walkthrough and live setups)

- **The confirmation code doesn't show:** refresh Settings. It arrives within
  a few seconds of clicking **Proceed** in Gmail.
- **The forwarding option is greyed out at work:** their admin blocks external
  forwarding. Suggest a filter from a personal account, or forwarding only
  specific emails.
- **Forwarding is set up but there's no list yet:** the first list covers mail
  received *after* forwarding was turned on, at the next delivery time.
